Triplet — Short Address Backfill

Gives every saved place a short address (“street number · area”, e.g. Jægersborggade 57 · Nørrebro) for the redesigned popup. For each place with no stored address details it asks OpenStreetMap (Nominatim) once, at most one request a second: first a search for the place's saved address (kept only if it lands within 250 m of the pin), otherwise a reverse lookup at the pin. Nothing is written until you press Write, and only places still missing details are written. Lookups are remembered in this browser, so you can close the page and come back: it picks up where it stopped.
