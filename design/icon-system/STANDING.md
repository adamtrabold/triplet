# Icons: standing decisions

Check every icon and every board against this list. Latest decision wins; update this file the same day the owner decides something.

## System and grid
- Build one harmonious, complete set plus rules that make future icons come out right first time. [10-21]
- The set covers place icons, app/UI icons and the Home Screen icon. [10-16]
- The grid and drawing approach are part of the brand, specific to Triplet; ignore how other systems size icons. [10-20]
- The grid governs the interior too, not only outer size and space to the ring. [10-16]
- Settle size, scale and weight at the grid level for all icons; never negotiate them icon by icon. [10-20]
- Use programmatic rules so every icon ever made is perfect; keep them flexible, not over-specific. [10-26]
- Draw each icon at the size it ships; 18 is the master; adjust detail for 24 and 12. [10-27]
- iPhone is the core output: the base system produces crisp icons for it, with no per-device refit pass. Size-specific standards are fine. [10-27]
- Make icons as sharp as possible; core dimensions and core line landings sit on whole pixels. [10-26]
- Start from the drawings as they were before pixel fitting, then snap by a rule for which side snaps, exterior form first. [10-27]
- Center vertically by eye, per icon. [10-22]
- Every icon has the same optical weight and scale in the set. [10-20]
- Icons are drawn by an expert icon designer at the highest level of glyph craft. [10-16]
- Batch a few icons at a time and pressure-test the grid as it is built. [10-17]
- Open: whether built still feels novel against the app's vintage-travel theme, or needs pushing. [10-15]
- Open: when an icon is outlined vs solid needs a logic; today place icons are solid in a stroked ring, outlines mean not yet tapped by me (star, visited). [10-25]

## Weight and strokes
- Strokes are 1.5px. [10-26]
- Parts that are not strokes need not be 1.5px; they must read at the right weight. [10-26]
- Nothing thinner than 1.25px. [10-27]
- Open: the badge ring reads slightly heavy. [10-27]
- Outline icons (star, +) match the set's visual weight; the pixel number is irrelevant. [10-26]
- Outline-icon counters must not feel cramped; balance stroke against counter. [10-23]
- Thin slabs (saucer bottom, museum beam) are shapes, not lines, and never thinner at the ends than the middle. [10-26]
- Counters are shapes on whole pixels, 1 or 2px. [10-26]
- Same-role parts are the same width (tines and the counters between them). [10-27]
- Line style sits between H2 and H3: loosened, humanist curves, restrained flares; monoweight stays a candidate. [10-21]

## Construction (roots, joins, bevels, counters)
- Every part comes from a simple root shape; roots join or separate to make the glyph. [10-23]
- Root shapes are proportionally related to each other and to the grid. [10-24]
- Align root shapes across icons; don't mix trapezoid, circle and square bases at random. [10-20]
- Build the brand into the root shapes; an icon looks like the system through the decisions that made it. [10-26]
- Minimal bezier points, perfect architecture. [10-16]
- A curved edge is drawn whole from one circle or ellipse, never one arc per point. [10-23]
- Holes are defined by proportional root shapes that also make the solid around them correct, not by stroke offsets. [10-26]
- A counter's curve follows the outside curve it sits in. [10-22]
- Round every meeting point at least slightly, some more; vary radius by how the angle meets. [10-20]
- Tight interior corners are slightly rounded, less than exterior corners; inner joins are never over-rounded. [10-21]
- Rounds follow how the part would really be machined smooth. [10-26]
- Use the bevel (museum capitals, plinth ends into columns). [10-26]
- One join pattern for each corner type across all icons. [10-22]
- Joins are smooth and architectural: no humps or wobbles at the join or on the line leaving it. [10-23]
- Every joint and flare is architecturally sound. [10-26]
- Handles get a rule from the root shape a handle is (cup vs bag). [10-26]
- No narrow passages that vanish at small sizes. [10-19]
- Open: how structural icons (museum) carry the flares the curved ones have. [10-21]

## Each icon
- Café: cup and saucer, no extras; the handle never pinches closed at the bottom. [10-19]
- Bar: coupe on a long stem; shape B, shallow pancaked bowl, wider at the bottom; delicate, never wine glass or chalice. [10-21]
- Coupe: clear band above the drink equals the stroke, or slightly thinner; the drink never looks empty. [10-26]
- Coupe: bowl sides curve, not straight and upright under the rim; foot top edge from one ellipse. [10-23]
- Open: the coupe interior changed in v282; check it against the approved one. [10-27]
- Museum: column front, three columns (two reads as a bank), wide enough on the grid for at least 1px counters. [10-27]
- Museum: flat base, separated gable, bevels; counters centered on whole pixels; it doesn't grow taller. [10-26]
- Open: three columns vs three counters; v282's version may work at 18. [10-27]
- Tree (Nature): conifer; the Between tree is fixed, the other icons adapt to it. [10-21]
- Tree: indented branches with light swoops, not plain triangles; tip rounding scales with its tier; top tier stays attached. [10-21]
- Fork and knife (Restaurant): the knife edge sticks out enough to see, set by rule; tines and counters one width. [10-27]
- Star: outline; same weight and size as the set; not approved. [10-27]
- Star: the row star and the tag star are one star. [10-26]
- +: same visual weight as the set; exactly centered; same whole-pixel width and height; not approved. [10-27]
- Landmark: Nordic loop, may stay a heavy line. Attraction: Ferris wheel. Park: bench. Venue: curtain. Hotel: bed. Neighbourhood: city blocks. Street: walker. [10-16]
- Open: Shopping; none of the proposed icons work, the bag may be too common. [10-26]
- Categories: 11 pins (Restaurant, Café, Bar, Venue, Museum, Landmark, Attraction, Park, Nature, Shopping, Hotel) plus Neighbourhood and Street. [10-16]
- Visited does not change. [10-14]

## Brand
- Direction is built/constructed; clarity first. [10-15]
- Whimsy is wanted; built can be whimsical; craft matters most. [10-15]
- Warmth from subtle rounding and other sources; hand-made is never ruled out. [10-15]
- The feel is pressed or settled ink, slightly less sharp, not a sharp road-sign screen. [10-20]
- Cues: whimsy, tactility, joy of movement; tool-drawn root shapes, flares, swoops in the tree, travel-sign graphics. [10-26]
- Straightforward, not cheeky. [10-26]
- Personality comes from icon choice, slightly left of center (the coupe). [10-26]
- Glyphs sit in the stroked ring and also work on their own. [10-16]
- US recreational road signs are inspiration, never copied. [10-15]
- Craft bar: Apple symbols, Central, Nucleo, Lucide. Material and Phosphor are not good craft; keep them on boards for comparison. [10-21]

## Rejected (never do)
- Ink traps. [10-19]
- The swoop signature; the bracket; cheeky signatures. [10-26]
- A rounded or sagging building base; the museum lintel arch; shed beam and stepped eaves. [10-26]
- Pill-shaped column counters. [10-19]
- Fully rounded cup base and tree trunk (H2); H1. [10-21]
- Intense or awkward flares; a deep, stout coupe bowl. [10-21]
- A bun on the café. [10-15]
- Ink thinner than 1.25px; a 0.75px anything. [10-27]
- A two-column museum. [10-27]
- An off-center +. [10-26]
- Star or + inside a badge ring. [10-27]
- Per-size, per-device refit passes; the v259 heavier-weight options. [10-27]
- Unequal widths for same-role parts. [10-27]
- Test icons Triplet doesn't need (the heart). [10-26]

## How he reviews
- On the design canvas icon board, every icon side by side; no separate pages or stills. [10-19]
- Boards show the real SVGs inline at true size, never images or blow-ups. [10-27]
- Show icons at the size they really are in the app, and in the app; star and + without rings. [10-27]
- Show options in context with the rest of the set. [10-21]
- Keep a master grid board and a construction overlay. [10-23]
- The canvas holds Start here plus one concise board per open topic; archive superseded boards the same round. [10-27]
- Give more than one option, and always keep the baseline option. [10-20]
- Close work gets small changes, not sweeping ones. [10-20]
- A question is a question and an opinion is not an order. [10-21]
- He checks on iPhone and a 5K display. [10-26]
- Notes are plain directives, with no sources. [10-27]
