# Icon system: the jobs

What the icons have to let you do. Every concept gets checked against this
list, and a concept fails if any job can't be done.

**Your words:** "they're too noisy… the checked items are stronger visually
than the open ones. the icon system needs more clarity... i need a full
rev." · "i want to see every icon in the app up next to each other."

## Jobs

1. **See what's left.** Look at the map or the list: the places you haven't
   been to come forward and the ones you've done step back. This has to
   hold on day one, halfway through the trip and on the last day.
2. **Tell open from done at a glance**: on the map near and far, in the
   list, in the tag, without done winning the eye.
3. **Scan without noise.** Marks don't compete with names or with each
   other. One state = one mark.
4. **One family.** Every icon looks drawn by the same hand, at the same
   weight. One ×, one +, and no stray characters standing in for icons.
5. **See them all side by side.** Every icon and every state, today next to
   the new one, on the shared canvas.
6. **Choose between concepts.** More than one real option.
7. **Know what kind of place it is,** in every state. Visiting a place
   never hides its category. *(inferred from "preserve the category icon")*
8. **Find your starred places,** especially the starred ones you haven't
   done yet. *(inferred)*
9. **Tell buttons from labels.** In the tag, the not-yet Star and Visited
   still look tappable, never greyed out. *(inferred)*
10. **See a change land.** Star or visit something and the mark confirms it
    with the motion you approved (stamp, dry-up), then settles quietly.
    *(inferred)*

## The walks (how each concept is checked)

| Walk | Steps | Proves |
|---|---|---|
| **Glance at the map** | open the app → map at three moments (day one, halfway, last day) → blur it → what does the eye hit first? | 1, 2, 3, 8 |
| **Zoom out** | pinch out → far pins and clusters → can you still tell open from done, and find a starred to-do? | 1, 2, 8 |
| **Scan the list** | read 5 rows (done, starred done, selected, open, approx street) | 2, 3, 7 |
| **Visit a place** | open its tag → tap Visited → watch it land on tag and pin → close | 9, 10 |
| **Visit from the list** | swipe a row left → it lands on the row, then on the pin | 10 |
| **Change your mind** | tap Visited again → it dries up → back to open | 10 |
| **Star something** | tap Star in the tag (or swipe a row right) → it inks in | 8, 9, 10 |
| **Review the set** | open the canvas → every icon, today vs new, grouped by family | 4, 5, 6 |

All of it runs on the same 27 real Reykjavík places for every concept
(`concepts/fixture/`), so the concepts compare like for like.
