Stroke width, 0.8 to 2.5px

You said"A feels to be producing better sized icons but the icons are too simplistic that are coming out in reality. Let's try a bunch of pixel widths for the stroke to give us the perfect overall density goal. I want sub pixels from prolly .8 to 2.5"

"Also include app icons not just the ones for types"

Eight widths: 0.8px, 1px, 1.25px, 1.5px, 1.75px, 2px, 2.25px, 2.5px. At each width, everything is rebuilt, not scaled:

  • The 13 place glyphs, solid, on grid A (the 12px box; TOP, SHOULDER, MIDDLE and BASE). Every line, every cut and the badge's ring take that width.
  • Finer strokes carry more detail. Repeated parts are "as many as fit": from 5 tines down to 2, 5 columns to 3, 8 spokes to 4, 3 slats to 2, 5 tiers to 3, a 4 × 4 block plan to 2 × 2, scallops on the curtain, a drink line in the coupe, a rim on the cup, a fold in the blanket. That's how the glyphs get richer, not only thinner.
  • The app's own icons (the +, the ×, account, star, locate, sort, filters, directions, the tick, the chevron, more), at the size each renders in the app, with that width. Closed shapes are solid (the account's head, locate's centre, the filter knobs, the dots); open strokes stay strokes; the Star is the only outline. Directions keeps a ring around its solid needle, the way a badge's ring holds a solid glyph.
  • Today's home-screen icon, unchanged, at its home-screen size (60px) in every block, so you can see it with each width. Its marks are 3.75px wide at that size, about a 2px line relative to its seal. Flag: as it stands it doesn't sit naturally in the family at any width. It's the only solid, scalloped, ringless mark: it pairs with the + button's scallop, not with the ringed badges, and it's the heaviest thing in every block.
  • Ink: how much of a 24px badge is ink, averaged over the 13. It's one number for "overall density".
  • Fixed: grid A's box, guides and the middle rule; the live circle Ø16; the corner rule (by joint, scaled with the width).

Guidance for the next icon

You asked for "an harmonious complete set and guidance for the future". These are the written rules every glyph on these sheets is built from. Whatever width you pick, they stay the same; only w changes.

  • Box. 12 × 12px: 6 to 18 in the 24px badge. The 12px chip is the same box with no ring.
  • Guides. TOP 6, SHOULDER 10, MIDDLE 12, BASE 18. A glyph with a ground stands on BASE; one without (cup, bed, blocks, ⌘) is centred on MIDDLE.
  • One width, w. Every line, the ring, and the app icons' strokes (at the size each renders) are w.
  • Cuts and spaces. A cut between touching parts is 0.6w (at least 0.6px); a space between separate parts is w (at least 1px).
  • Repeated parts: as many as fit with those cuts and spaces (tines, columns, spokes, slats, tiers, blocks).
  • Small details (a rim, a drink line, a fold, an architrave, a book's band) only at w ≤ 1.5px.
  • Solid or outline (the team's rule, from yours: "the pin/place icons need to be solid and outlined icons should be used when something has yet to be tapped by me like Star and visited"): closed shapes are solid; open strokes stay strokes; only the Star and Visited are outlines. A ring may hold a solid mark (the badge, Directions).
  • Angles: orthogonal, 45°, whole-pixel slopes. Corners: by joint, radii scaled with w (the corner law is a later pick).
Test: a new icon, built only from the rules above

"Library" is not one of the 13 and nobody asked for it. It's here to test whether the rules alone make a new icon that sits with the set: books standing on BASE, as many as fit, spines between SHOULDER and TOP, one leaning at 45°, a band on each spine where w ≤ 1.5.

Result: in weight, size and detail it sits with the set at every width. As an idea it reads as a bar chart. The rules make an icon consistent; they don't make its concept clear. That still needs the concept step.
