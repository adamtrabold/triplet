H1: monoweight, eased curves
A CURVE RULE for every icon: each outside round and join starts 2.3 times farther from its corner and eases out of the line with no jump in curvature, a hand's stroke rather than a compass arc; the corner is about as deep as before. Rules that bend

The corner law keeps its radii in every tuning; H1 to H3 change only how a round meets its lines (eased, not a compass arc).

Interior tighter than exterior (your rule) bends in H3 only, at one kind of corner: where a thin post flares into a mass. Counters, slots and the tree's notches stay tight in every tuning.

Every base is a slab gains one exception in H2 and H3: a foot that holds something up by a stem is a DOME (the coupe now; a lamp, a trophy, a bell later). The saucer and the plinth stay slabs.

Monoweight (your pick) holds in H1 and H2; H3 relaxes it for free-standing posts only, and says so in its name.

The Outline class: in H1 to H3 the star's points reach as far as the +'s arms (the shipped star's 4px hole minimum made it 15% wider than v133); its hole is then about 2 lines.
