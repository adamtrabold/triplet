The eight kinds of root
Mass. The thing itself (cup, tier, blade, mattress): 2 lines thick (3px).
Slab. Carries or caps (beam, saucer, foot, roof): 1 line (1 1/2px).
Post. Holds up (stem, column, trunk, leg): 5/6 line; flares half a pixel into what it holds.
Loop. A band round a counter (the cup's ear, a bag's handle): 5/6 line.
Hole. A proportional shape cut from a root (the star's middle, the coupe's band).
Notch. Where a tier sits in the one below: only the small round, so it never fills.
Capital. A post holding a slab past its end: the slab's end drops into a quarter ellipse.
Tip. A point goes soft by a share of its shorter edge (the tree, the star).
Each picture is a close-up of one of the drawings above.
