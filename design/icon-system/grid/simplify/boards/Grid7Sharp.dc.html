The rules
•
Shapes are whole pixels. A slab (beam, plinth, saucer, foot) is 2px; its flat edges and lowest point land whole.
•
The line stays 1½px (posts, walls, rims, stems): one edge whole, the other on a half pixel.
•
Counters are whole pixels, at least 2px; gaps at least 1px. One exception (coupe C), from your 10-21 words: a band under a rim "equal to the thickness of the stroke", 1½px. Your latest, on v264, asked the other way: "maybe they could go up to 2px?" (A, B).
•
No ink under 1½px, but a cap's ends, where its curve runs out (1px: the cap rule, kept).
•
What must stick out sticks out at least 1px (the knife's edge, now 1px past its handle, as on v266; a lip). A bevel is not a protrusion: the museum's ½px overhang is all curve, column into slab (your "bevel of the plinth ends into the columns"), so it stays ½px, its ends on a half pixel.
•
Symmetric icons sit on the centre line, or ¼px off it when a 1½px part is centred, so that part keeps one whole edge like every other line (museum B¼, the + at 1½).
•
The star and the + are drawn 1:1 where they live, sized by their neighbours, never in a ring.
