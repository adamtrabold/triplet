The rule
1
Lines and parts are whole device pixels per screen: the 1½px line is 3 on the 5K and 5 on an iPhone (1⅔px); a post is 2 and 4, so it stays thinner. The badge ring takes the line's width.
2
Every straight edge, and the outermost point of every big curve, lands on a whole device pixel.
3
Optical centring snaps too: the glyph moves less than half a device pixel. Symmetric glyphs stay symmetric.
4
Curves, diagonals, tips and corner rounds stay where they were drawn. The edges around them carry them, so they never bend.
5
It runs per size and per screen, like the @2x and @3x files an iPhone app ships, as a last pass after the drawing.
What it costs

The line is 1½px on the 5K and 1⅔px on an iPhone, 11% heavier there. Centring moves at most ⅓px (the + on the 5K moves ¼px across and ¼px down). Short lengths move by up to a device pixel: the coupe's band is ½px on the 5K, and the museum's capital steps get shorter, so their flares turn a little more sharply.
