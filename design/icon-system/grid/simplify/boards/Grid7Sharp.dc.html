The rules
•
Shapes are whole pixels. A slab (beam, plinth, saucer, foot) is 2px, the first whole pixel not thinner than the line; its flat edges and a curve's flat side and lowest point land whole.
•
The line stays 1½px (posts, walls, rims, stems): one edge whole, the other on a half pixel.
•
Counters are whole pixels, at least 2px (openings, the coupe's band, the cup's ear). Gaps between parts: whole, at least 1px.
•
No ink under 1½px. Only a cap's ends, where its curve runs out, reach 1px (half its 2px middle: the cap rule, kept).
•
What must stick out sticks out at least 1px (the knife's edge, a step, a lip): the join's ½px round eats the first ½px.
•
Symmetric icons stay on the centre line; a part centred on it lands whole only at an even width.
•
The star and the + are drawn 1:1 where they live, sized by what sits beside them, never in a ring.
