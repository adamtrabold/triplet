The rules, as they now stand
•
Shapes are whole pixels. A slab (beam, plinth, saucer, foot) is 2px; its flat edges and lowest point land whole.
•
The line stays 1½px (posts, walls, rims, stems): one edge whole, the other on a half pixel.
•
Counters and gaps are whole pixels, at least 1px (your v269 "1px minimum counter space"; was "at least 2", with coupe C as an exception: both gone). A counter is what the drawing's proportions give, rounded to a whole pixel.
•
No ink under 1½px, but a cap's ends (1px, the cap rule). What must stick out sticks out at least 1px (the knife's edge); a bevel is not a protrusion (the museum's ½px).
•
Symmetric icons sit exactly on the centre line, never off it ("off center is not acceptable"). A 1½px part on the centre goes soft instead. The ¼px rule is withdrawn (B¼, the ¼-off +).
•
Line marks are drawn 1:1 where they live, one drawing per mark (one star, 12px, row and tag), their stroke set by eye against the whole set, not by pixel count ("actual pixel is irrelevant").
