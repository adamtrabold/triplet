Icon grid · the one grid

The grid

One grid, every icon drawn on it: 1px cells in a 24 frame, the centre on a grid line. Every solid part lands on lines; strokes (the + and the star) are 1½px, one edge on a line and one on the half line. The six below are built on it by the engine, and the check reads every edge: 0 of 60 off the grid (approved: 61 of 65).

1 · The grid
Cell
1px
the only place sharp on both your 5K (2x) and an iPhone (3x).
Centre
a line
12 is a grid line, so anything centred is an even width.
Solid
2px
every solid part (slab, post, column, stem, trunk, rim, wall, ear): the first whole px not thinner than the 1½ stroke.
Gap
1px
the space between two parts: a cut, an opening, the coupe's band.
Counter
1 or 2
a hole in one part: what its root leaves; at least 2 along, or the root grows a px (1 × 1 reads as a dot).
Stroke
1½px
the + and the star only: one edge on a line, one on the half line. Sharp on the 5K; on an iPhone the half edge is soft (the price of 1½).
Overhang
1px
what sticks out does so by a whole px. The capital's ½ bevel is one curve: no edge to land.
Point
+½
an apex or tip may pass its box by ½ (a point carries less ink).
Boxes
10 · 11 × 9 · 8 × 11
each class's size limit: the approved size to whole px. Marks: star 12 tall, + 13½ across, 1:1 where they live.
2 · How each kind of part lands
Slab
beam, plinth: 2 tall, both faces on lines. A cap (saucer, foot): crown 2, ends 1, half the crown.
Post
column, stem, trunk, rim, wall: 2 wide, on lines. Centred, it sits 11..13.
Gap · counter
a gap is 1. A counter is 1 or 2 across and at least 2 along.
Curve
its extremes (a bowl's bottom, an ellipse's side, a tier's lowest point) on lines. Where a straight cut meets it, the corner sits on the curve.
Point · diagonal
tips, apexes, roof pitches and the small rounds at corners are free: they have no edge to land.
A row of equal parts
centred, the thing on the centre is even. 2px parts in an odd count take 1px gaps (the middle part on the centre); an even count takes 2px gaps (the middle gap on it).
Protrusion
whole px, at least 1 (the knife's edge, an overhang).
Proportions
every ratio stays (proportions.js). Its result goes to the nearest whole px. If the box can't hold it, the box wins.
Placement
an icon moves only in whole px: up or down to its optical centre; a symmetric icon on the centre line; a lopsided one (the cup) to the whole px nearest its optical centre.
A centred stroke
its centre on a quarter line (k + ¼), so each edge lands on a line or a half line. The + is drawn so; its frame's centre is its button's centre, so it is dead centre in the orange circle.
3 · Your museum question: three columns, centred

Width. Columns 2, openings 1 (a gap between parts): 2 + 1 + 2 + 1 + 2 = 8; the beam and plinth pass it by 1 each side: 10, the square box. The middle column sits 11..13, on the centre.

Not four: an even count puts an opening on the centre, so openings of 2: 4 × 2 + 3 × 2 + 2 = 16, past the box.

Height. Plinth 2, columns 3 (⅓ of the colonnade: 2⅔ → 3), beam 2, gap 1, gable ends 1, roof 1½ to the apex: 10½, the box's 10 plus the apex's ½.

Why it is taller than approved (9.6 → 10.5): the beam and plinth were 1.04 each and are now 2 (+1.9); the columns 2.6 → 3 (+0.4). The box takes back 1.4 from the roof (its rise 1⅔ → 1½, the ends 1.2 → 1). It is wider by 0.2 (9.8 → 10, the nearest even).

20 of 20 edges on lines, sharp on both screens; only the roof's slopes and apex are free.
