Set by the designer, yours to change
  • Stroke at 18 on the grid: 1 1/2px, its outer side on the grid and its inner side half an iPhone pixel soft (1 1/3 was sharper both sides but read lighter than the set's weight).
  • Thinnest part at 12: A row's parts may thin to 2/3px (two iPhone pixels) at 12 to keep the row's count; nothing else goes under 1px.
  • The ring: 1 1/3px at 24 (4 iPhone pixels): whole pixels and a step lighter than the glyph's stroke.
  • When rules meet: Continuous curvature first, then minimum widths, then same-role widths, then protrusions, then the grid. The box always holds.
  • Chamfer angle: 45 degrees: the cut rises as far as it runs, half above the post and half into it (a steeper cut turns under 30 degrees at its edges and reads as a knee). At 18 the third of a line lands at 2/3px.
  • The rack at 24 and 12: The cutters are set at 18 and scale with the drawing at 24 and 12, so every size is the same icon.
  • Star: The v264 star at the row's 18px box (one star for the row and the tag); its outline 1 1/3px.
  • +: The v264 + at 14px across in the 60px button, centred on a whole px, its stroke 2px (6 iPhone pixels, so both sides land); same whole-pixel width and height.
2 · The system, in six lines
  1. An icon is a declaration and nothing else: its roots top to bottom, each a kind of root (vessel, loop, cap, slab, post, gable, tiers, row, comb, head, blade) sized as a share of a parent, each meeting the one above it: rests on (a cut), into (a fillet), held under an overhang (a chamfer), on a wall (one tangent curve), stacked (notched).
  2. The box: fitted to its class (wide, square, tall) by the sides that scale; never more than one iPhone pixel past its v264 size.
  3. The grid: straight widths and overhangs land on the iPhone pixel before anything is drawn on them; the roots land silhouette first; a row or a comb lands as one, its parts and gaps equal.
  4. Rows and combs: parts at the size's part width, gaps equal and at least the counter minimum; parts thin before a part is lost.
  5. Machined: every round is a cutter from the rack (1/8, 1/4, 1/2px at 18, scaled at 24 and 12); a post under an overhang meets it with a 45° chamfer, its edges broken; a post into a face with the 1/2 fillet; tips broken by their edge.
  6. Curvature continuous through every join. Every check runs on every size and a failure blocks publishing.
Every setting, with its date, is in THE-SYSTEM.md.
