The master grid

Everything a new icon is drawn from, in one place, drawn from the code the icons are built from (src/proportions.js, the engine's own record of the six), so it changes when a rule does. px are pixels at the 24px badge. The full spec, with every rule and the steps, is at the top of Construction, beside this board.

The badge, the coin, the lattice

The badge is the seal; the glyph is struck inside it like a coin. Ring 1 1/2px · 2px of air · live circle Ø16 · the coin Ø12 in the 12px box. Grid: 1px. Faint: the 1/3px lattice. The lattice places a part's first edge. A thickness that is a part (a line, post, slab) is exact and its far edge may sit off the lattice; a space or a shown length (a cut, a gap, how much of a post shows) is a minimum, and the next part starts on the first lattice line at least that far away.

A horizontal block that carries or caps something is a slab, one line; a block that is the thing itself is a mass, a shape. Test: if it still names the thing drawn one line thick, it's a slab. Every curved edge is part of one root: a vessel's ellipse, a cap's, a circle. A counter is a root too: the same shape made smaller, the space a spacing leaves, or a root moved.

Shaped marks flare where a post holds a mass; line marks have no masses, so no flares and lighter mass corners. Inside corners are tighter than outside ones; no ink traps.

The line

Monoweight: one line, 1 1/2px, the ring's weight, everywhere, horizontals and verticals alike. Posts are 5/6 of it (1 1/4px), the resting cut 2/3 (1px), a step 1/3 (1/2px), a clear band 1/2 (3/4px). Outline marks keep the line; the open mark (the +) carries 10/9 of it, 1 2/3px, set by darkness.

Stacked

A thing of tiers takes the tree as its template: tiers showing 4.83 : 3 : 3.33 of the height, tip to base; half widths 2.8 : 4.1 : 5.3 (the bottom one the class's); each notch 3/5 of the half width of the tier above; living things droop 1/2 line and sweep in 1/5 line, buildings stay straight; the trunk shows one line. These are his chosen rhythm, off the series on purpose.
