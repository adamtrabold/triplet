Line weight
One line, the ring's weight. A hole's inside runs parallel to its outside at that weight (monoweight). Two real tapers are on the Line board for you; line marks reading lighter than the solid places is on the Weight board.
Stacked gables
All by rule: three tiers of equal height (h); every tier the same gable (one slope); every notch shelf one and a half lines (2.25px), so a fir reads as a fir at 100%; the bottom tier spans the tall class (9 1/3); the trunk shows one line; the apex passes TOP by 1/2. Inside rounds tighter than outside.
