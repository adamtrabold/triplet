Curvature: what was wrong, and the fix

v142 built the band's sides as a true offset: every point exactly a line from the bowl, measured square to it. But across the band the bowl turns, and a true offset bends tighter than the curve it follows, so its sides leaned in faster than the bowl. Read straight across, the way the eye reads an upright wall, the wall grew from 1.54 at the band's top to 1.67 at its bottom.

Now each side of the band is the bowl's own curve over the band's height, moved in by the line: the same curve, with the same curvature, as the outside. The orange line is the outside moved in by exactly one line (1.5px), drawn over the band at 8x.

A light gap between dark lines reads wider than it measures, so the band is 1 1/6px (was 1 1/3; the stroke is 1.5). It also puts the drink's surface back on the 1/3px grid (1.5 + 1 1/6 = 2 2/3). If it should go thinner still, 1px is beside it.
