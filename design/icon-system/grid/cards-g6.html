The grid, pressure-tested

You said "Let's do a few at a time— we will need to pressure test the grid as it's being made". First, icon by icon, where grid A held and where it broke. Then each change we propose, shown before and after. Batch 1 is the first, so there are no earlier drawings to re-check; batch 2 will re-check these six under any new change.

Held and broke, icon by icon
  • Café. Held: box; MIDDLE (the cup and saucer centres on it exactly); the ear opens 2.3px at 24. Broke: cut 0.9 → change 1; the rim line → change 3; curves had no rule → change 4.
  • Bar. Held: box; TOP (the rim) and BASE (the foot); SHOULDER (the bowl ends on it); one piece, no cuts. Broke: the drink line → change 3; curves → change 4.
  • Museum. Held: box; TOP, SHOULDER (the beam), BASE (the plinth); the pediment's 1-in-2 slope; the beam is a part, not a cut, so it stays. Broke: cut 0.9 → change 1; its own column spacing → change 2; lopsided slot ends → change 7.
  • Nature. Held: box; TOP (the tip), BASE (the trunk). Broke: branches are curves, which the angle rule didn't cover → change 5; the g4 tier table says four at 1.5px, and four crowd at 24 → change 8.
  • The +. Held: its size and place in the + badge. Broke: a stroke can't take a corner law → change 6.
  • The Star. Held: the one outline; its size. Broke: a stroke → change 6.
The changes (proposed; none is a one-icon exception). Change 7 was added after the director's score.

Change 1: a cut is 1px, not 0.9

0.9 (0.6 × the line) never lands on the 0.25px drawing lattice, so every edge after a cut drifted off it. 1px does. It fixes the café and the museum; at real size the difference is a hair (the museum, real size and 2x, below).

Change 2: spaces between repeated parts = the line

The skeleton gave the museum its own wider column spacing, an exception for one icon. The general rule (a space between separate parts is the line, 1.5px) fits four columns, and four reads as a column front at 24px.

Own spacing 2.1px: 3 columns

Change 3: no hairline cuts inside a mass

This replaces "small details only at w ≤ 1.5px", which let in the rim, the drink line, the bag's band and the bed's fold; all four cost the read (g5). Detail comes from the silhouette and from open counters instead. Shown on the two in this batch; the bag and bed are batch 2.

Change 4: a rule for curves

The grid had none. As drawn: a curve runs between two points that are its extremes, with flat or upright handles, and ends on the 0.25px lattice or at a corner. So a bowl is two curves. Squares are points; dots are handles.

Change 5: angles: straight lines and curves

Straight lines keep "orthogonal, 45° or a whole-pixel slope". The conifer's branches are curves, which that rule didn't cover; they follow change 4.

Curved: each branch is one curve

Change 6: app icons are drawn as shapes

The + and the Star are drawn like the places, so the corner law reaches them. A stroke can only have round or square ends.

Change 7: two rounds that collide become one arc

Added after the director's score. Where two rounds want more room than a short edge has (a 1.5px slot end, a bar's end), batch 1 dropped the leftover run, which left the slot's top off centre (x 9.03 on a slot centred at 9.0) with unequal curves. The rule now: they become one true semicircle, radius 0.75 on a 1.5px slot, drawn as two equal quarter arcs meeting on the centre line. Any other two rounds that would leave a run under 0.2px share the edge evenly. Shown 68x on one slot of the museum (squares are points, dots are handles), then real size before and after. Re-checked under it, all six:

  • Café: by joint 0 arcs, even wear 1 arc, pressed 0 arcs. Mirror check: not symmetric by design (the ear). Slivers: 0.
  • Bar: by joint 0 arcs, even wear 0 arcs, pressed 0 arcs. Mirror check: worst 0.000px. Slivers: 0.
  • Museum: by joint 6 arcs, even wear 2 arcs, pressed 6 arcs. Mirror check: worst 0.001px. Slivers: 0.
  • Nature: by joint 0 arcs, even wear 1 arc, pressed 0 arcs. Mirror check: worst 0.001px. Slivers: 0.
  • The +: by joint 0 arcs, even wear 4 arcs, pressed 0 arcs. Mirror check: worst 0.000px. Slivers: 0.
  • The Star: by joint 0 arcs, even wear 0 arcs, pressed 0 arcs. Mirror check: worst 0.000px. Slivers: 0.

The mirror check: every point has a mirrored twin within 0.01px (the batch 1 museum missed by 0.06px). The checks are in src/check-svg.py, beside the sliver scan.
