Four places and two app icons, drawn properly on grid A at 1.5px. Each one tests a different kind of joint, so the grid gets pushed in different places:

  • Café: curves. A low, wide cup, its handle an ear, on a saucer with a lifted rim.
  • Bar: a curve flowing into a straight stem, and a foot.
  • Museum: right angles and a repeated part (columns).
  • Nature: sharp tips and notches.
  • The + and the Star: a crossing, and the one outline.

Every part is a simple shape (rectangle, triangle, a curve with two points), joined into one solid glyph. The bowls of the cup and the coupe are each two curves; the conifer's drooping branches are one curve each side.

Your build brief"they need to sit in the stroked circle but also make sense on their own. they need to be at their root simple shapes, but joined together to make coherent glyphs. they must be crafted with the highest attention to detail and quality -- minimal bezier curves with perfect architecture. personality in the approach to how strokes are connected and curve. slight rounding of interior and exterior corners to add a vintage softness. corner rounding should not be 100% consistent though as there are different architecturally sound prinicples to how different angles intersect. the effect of the rounding should allude to something screened or pressed."

What you can judge now that you couldn't on skeletons: the corner law (sheet 2, on the drawings; sheet 3 in the app) and the air in the ring (sheet 4). Sheet 5 is the grid, updated by what this batch found.

Not in this batch: the other 9 places and the rest of the app icons. Visited and the home-screen icon are unchanged.

The grid, after batch 1

You said "Let's do a few at a time— we will need to pressure test the grid as it's being made". These are grid A's rules as they stand after drawing this batch. Kept held up; Changed is where a drawing fought the rule and we propose changing the rule rather than bending the drawing; Open is what the next batch has to test.

  • KeptLine: 1.5px, everything: every line, the ring, the app icons at the size each renders.
  • KeptBox and guides: 12 × 12 in the 24 badge. TOP 0 and BASE 12 did their job in all four: the coupe's rim, the pediment and the tree's tip sit on TOP; the foot, plinth and trunk stand on BASE.
  • KeptMIDDLE: a glyph with no ground of its own is centred on MIDDLE. The cup and saucer sits exactly on it.
  • ChangedCut between touching parts: 1px, not 0.9. 0.9 (0.6 × the line) never lands on the drawing lattice, so every edge after a cut drifted off it. 1px does, and it opens a little more at 24px: the cup above its saucer, the pediment above the beam.
  • ChangedSpaces between repeated parts = the line (1.5px). The skeleton gave the columns their own wider spacing (2.1px), which allowed three. The general rule allows four, and four reads as a column front at 24px. Proposed: no special spacing for one icon.
  • ChangedNo hairline cuts inside a mass. The rule "small details only at w ≤ 1.5px" let in the cup's rim, the drink line, the bag's band and the bed's fold, and all four cost the read (g5). Proposed: detail comes from the silhouette and from open counters (the cup's ear, the gaps between columns), not from cuts through a solid.
  • ChangedCurves: the grid had no rule for them. Proposed, as drawn here: a curve runs between two points that are its extremes (flat or upright handles), so a bowl is two curves; a curve ends on the 0.25px lattice or at a corner.
  • ChangedAngles: straight lines keep "orthogonal, 45° or a whole-pixel slope" (the pediment rises 1 in 2). The conifer's branches are curves, which that rule didn't cover; they follow the curve rule.
  • ChangedApp icons are drawn, not stroked. The + and the Star are shapes with the same corner law as the places. A stroke can't take a corner law (its ends are either round or square).
  • OpenSHOULDER (4) isn't doing work yet. No part of this batch lands on it: the coupe's bowl ends at 4.6, the museum's beam at 3.9. Batch 2 tests it on the hotel, venue and landmark; if they don't use it either, we propose retiring it.
  • OpenThe corner law and the air: your picks (sheets 2 to 4).
  • OpenPixel lattice: every point sits on a 0.25px lattice. A 1.5px line centred in the box can't sit on whole or half pixels (the coupe's stem runs 11.25 to 12.75 in the badge), so its edges fall between dots on a 3x phone. To settle with the corner law.

Next batch, if this direction is right: hotel (the bed without its fold), venue, landmark (the ⌘) and attraction (the wheel's spokes), with the account and locate icons.
