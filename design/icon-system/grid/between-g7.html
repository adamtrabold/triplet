Five rules, each a value for every icon. The tree follows all of them and comes out byte-identical; the coupe changes only in its drink.

RuleWasNow
PlatesH3's dome turned under: ends thin to a pointA plate departs from flat by 1/2px at the centre, so its ends stay 1px (2/3 of the line). What a thing sits on curves underneath, flat on top.
Here: Cup: the saucer. The coupe's foot: see the question below. Museum, tree: they stand on the ground, flat (unchanged).
Posts1 1/4px for a free post; the columns exemptEvery post that holds a mass up is 1 1/4px, columns included.
Here: Museum: the four columns, so each opening is 1/3px wider (the outside edges stay). Coupe stem, trunk: already. +: holds nothing up, 1 1/2.
Resting parts(no rule; the cup was cut from its saucer by the 1px cut)A load set on separate supports is cut from them by the 1px cut: the cup on its saucer, a roof on its columns. Parts made as one piece (a glass, a tree) join, and a post flares into the mass it grows into.
Here: Museum: a 1px cut under the roof; the columns stand on the base, joined, with square openings at their feet. Cup: as it was. Coupe, tree: one piece each, unchanged.
Straight-lined buildings(no rule)A building's walls, beams and roof slopes stay straight; sweep and droop are for living things. The 1/2px a plate rule gives a base and a vessel's curved side are not touched by this.
Here: Museum: the pediment stays straight. Tree: keeps its sweep and droop.
The clear band under a rim7/6px5/6px, so the drink's surface sits on the next 1/3px lattice line up. Still under the line, as you asked ("equal to the thickness of the stroke. or maybe even a little thinner").
Here: Coupe only. 1px was tried: at 24px it didn't show.
Unchanged: line ends, tips by their edge, flares 1/2px, mass corners 0.6, the vessel side, the tree's sweep and droop. The + and the star: nothing in these rules acts on them.
