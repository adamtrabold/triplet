Woodcut
Like wood left standing in a woodcut: an upright is stoutest where it meets the mass it holds up. Applies to every post that meets a mass (the coupe's stem, the columns, the trunk under its tier): straight through its middle, widening over the last fifth at each joined end by 1/12 of its length. 1.5px is the post at its narrowest.
