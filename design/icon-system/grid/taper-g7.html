The taper: three rules, and none

The question
The taper you liked on the coupe, made into a rule three ways, each from a real way of making a mark, with no taper at all on the top row. Which do you want, if any?

Counterpunch
Like the counterpunch a punchcutter strikes into metal type: a hole is the outside shape made smaller, so its wall is full where the shape bulges and thins where it narrows. Applies to holes cut into a solid: the coupe's band, the star's hole.

Engraver's burin
Like a line engraved into a signet ring: a line thins where it ends free, as the graver lifts out. Applies to free ends: the +'s arms, the trunk. Closed outlines stay even.

Woodcut
Like wood left standing in a woodcut: an upright is stoutest where it meets the mass it holds up. Applies to posts: the coupe's stem, the columns, the trunk.
