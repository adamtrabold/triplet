The line: monoweight, and two real tapers

The question
Real tapers now: in the two lower rows a line changes width smoothly from one end to the other and its inside keeps following the outside. Top row is monoweight. Which do you want?
The rule
True size
Large (8x): café, bar, museum, nature, +, star
Monoweight
Every line the same 1.5px. Every hole's inner edge runs parallel to its outer edge, so the inside follows the outside: the coupe's band, the cup's handle, the star's hole. Like neon tube.

Sign painter's flat brush
The brush is held flat, as on a road sign or a painted banner: a line is full (1.5px) where it runs up and down and thin (1px) where it runs across, changing smoothly around a curve. The handle is full at its back and thin at its arms; the star's arms change with their angle; every horizontal (saucer, foot, plinth, rim) is thin.

Settled ink
Ink on a hung, screened banner settles downward: a line is thin (1px) at its top and full (1.5px) at its bottom, changing smoothly down its length. The handle thins at its top arm, the star at its top point; posts (the stem, the columns, the trunk, the +) widen downward.
