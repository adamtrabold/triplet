## Key finding

Every state adds ink, and the base (open) state is the lightest mark. On the
map the visited sticker is the heaviest mark and erases the category glyph.
Starred stacks star + keyline + halo. The drawing language has 6 stroke
weights, 3 checks, 2 ×s, text-glyph icons (▼ ×, + as rotated ×), and
mismatched optical mass across the category glyphs.

## Proposed (not decided) principles

1. Weight follows what's left to do (open+starred > open > visited+starred ≥ visited).
2. Done differs in kind, not weight.
3. State never erases identity (category glyph survives).
4. One state, one mark, one place.
5. Open-as-absence in rows/pins; open-as-control in the tag must invite the tap.
