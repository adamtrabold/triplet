## Decisions I made in the jam (steer, not owner calls)

- Concepts differ in the **state model**, share **one grammar** (judged
  once, on its own board).
- Grammar = three voices by who made the mark: **Printed** (guide: category
  glyphs, numbers, counts; flat solids), **Inked by you** (visited stamp,
  star; the only voice with tilt/texture), **Line** (tool: every control,
  one stroke ladder, round caps/joins, one × / + / check, no text chars).
  Two checks with two jobs: the Line check (sort/plan) and the Inked check
  (visited).
- **Weather-map legend rule:** each concept states channel → meaning; no
  channel spent twice. Today: size = selected and zoom; dark fill = visited
  and selected; orange = star and cluster.
- Rule to put to the owner (shown, not argued): "At rest, a pin says one
  thing per channel; the character lives in how it changes and in the tag."
- Selection = navy on every surface (matches the navy selected row).
- Rim star (no halo or keyline) to be tested at 16px and on an orange OSM
  road.
- District/street: solid Printed glyphs that keep their idea (designer's
  call). "other": redraw (designer's call). Leaflet zoom: redraw in the Line
  voice; removing it is an owner question. Attribution flag: untouched.
- Tag: the open Star/Visited segments are Line-voiced controls, the done
  ones Inked. A ghost stamp for open risks reading "disabled" (= signed
  out). It must be shown beside the signed-out state.
- + / account badge containers belong to the neighbouring crews. We supply
  glyphs only.
