# Icon concepts: jobs

What each icon has to let you do or recognise, and what it must never be
mistaken for. This step decides **what each icon depicts and why**. It
doesn't decide the drawing: the grid and the drawings come next.
*Inferred* means we added it and you didn't say it.

From your words (2026-10-15): "the clarity is best with built … through
subtle rounding, etc. we can get some of the warmth / mimicry of the inspo
/ vintage roadtrip brochures/pamphlets/ etc. the rest risk feeling
amateurish … now let's get to deeper brainstorm on the icons themselves --
meaning the concept behind each icon, not the exact drawing."

## Settled (not reopened here)

- **Constructed** is the direction. In his words: "the clarity is best with
  built. i think through subtle rounding, etc. we can get some of the
  warmth / mimicry of the inspo / vintage roadtrip brochures/pamphlets/
  etc." The rest is in `../owner.md`, including "i dont want to say never
  to that" and "these are just examples. not exhaustive."
- **Outlined means you haven't marked it yet** (Star, Visited). Nothing else
  is an empty shape.
- Places are solid. Every line is 2.00px on screen. Owner on the circle and the build (verbatim, 10-16): "yes they need to sit in the stroked circle but also make sense on their own. they need to be at their root simple shapes, but joined together to make coherent glyphs. they must be crafted with the highest attention to detail and quality -- minimal bezier curves with perfect architecture. personality in the approach to how strokes are connected and curve. slight rounding of interior and exterior corners to add a vintage softness. corner rounding should not be 100% consistent though as there are different architecturally sound prinicples to how different angles intersect. the effect of the rounding should allude to something screened or pressed."
- **Visited doesn't change**: the stamp, the sticker and the mark-visited
  check.
- On hand-made, his words (2026-10-15): "i dont want to say never to that
  and handmade can come from many angles -- construction method, textural
  approach, the inkblot thing, etc." and "these are just examples. not
  exhaustive." On whimsy: "there is not one correct way to show whimsy
  though and the craft matters most".

## Jobs for the whole set

1. **See a concept for every icon in the app**, not a sample: the 13
   places (11 pins, 2 shapes), every tool, the + and the account (`../inventory.md`).
2. **Choose between at least two concepts per icon**, different in kind
   (not two drawings of one idea), each with why it fits a traveller.
3. **Know the risk of each**: what it could be mistaken for, on the map, in
   a row and at 12px.
4. **See every place concept against the real places you've saved**, not
   against the category's name alone. A cup has to work for a bakery and
   a cat café; a bar icon for a jazz club.
5. **See which category names are themselves unclear**, and decide whether
   to keep, rename, merge or split them before any icon is chosen for
   them. Inferred: an icon can't be clearer than the idea behind it.
6. *Inferred:* **One family**: the concepts together read as one printed
   set from one brochure, not a mix of clip-art sources.

## Tools: recognise the action instantly

| Tool | You need to recognise | Must not be mistaken for |
|---|---|---|
| + (add a place) | "add a new place" | zoom in; new plan; add to plan (all + today) |
| × on the + (add form open) | "close the form" | delete |
| Account | "me, signed in / sign in" | a contact, a person on the map |
| Sort | "change the order" | filter; up/down votes |
| Filters | "choose which kinds show" | settings, audio sliders |
| Locate | "show me where I am" | directions; a gun sight; you-are-here (the result) |
| Directions (tag) | "get directions there" | a turn sign; locate; empty-list compass |
| Tag close × | "close this tag" | delete the place |
| Row delete × | "delete this place" (destructive) | close; remove from plan |
| Plan remove × / add to plan + | "out of / into this plan" | delete; add a new place |
| Current-choice tick (sort, plan) | "this one is chosen" | **Visited's check (FIXED)** |
| More (…) | "more actions on this plan" | loading, an ellipsis in text |
| Chevron, collapse ▼ | "open / fold this" | sort; "go down" |
| Zoom + / − | "zoom" | add a place |
| Empty-list compass | "nothing here yet" | Directions |

Inferred: **the same symbol never means two things.** Today × means close,
delete and remove, and + means add a place, new plan, add to plan and zoom.
Concepts must say which meanings may share a symbol and which may not.
