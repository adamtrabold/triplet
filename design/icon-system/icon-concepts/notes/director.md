## My bias (keep checking it)

The director's scores (mine and my predecessor's) rewarded the hand-made
and the novel. He picks clarity and print. My scoring order this step:
(1) reads at real size (24 badge, 12 chip, map pin), (2) covers what he
actually saved, (3) one printed family, (4) only then wit or novelty. A
plain answer is never marked down. On a tie, the common sign wins.
