# Icon system: history

One entry per moment the owner saw work or decided. At most 4 lines each, with the deciding quote only. Full words are in `owner.md`, the team's reasoning in the `jam.md` and `scores.md` files. Review canvas: https://claude.ai/artifact/SVKZ9PpaMCKUpk7Ks622jW (older rounds are on its Archive page).

**Snapshots** (`snapshots/`): 1 start: the app today and every icon side by side (the inventory). The end shot gets added when the new icons land.


- **10-07 · The ask:** "they're too noisy … the checked items are stronger visually than the open ones … i need a full rev."
- **10-13 · Every icon side by side:** an inventory of 74 icons on the canvas. `inventory.md`
- **10-14 · False start:** the crew worked from an old backlog line. "Visited should not change. Also I never told you what I wanted changed" and "all I want is every icon side by side" → reset to brand approach first, then grid and SVGs. Lesson: brief from his current words.
- **10-14 · v4 brand directions:** "the pin/place icons need to be solid and outlined icons should be used when something has yet to be tapped by me" → that became the system rule. `brand-approach/concepts/sketches/v4-*`
- **10-15 · The + joins:** "Build the matched icon but we need to include this in the icon project everything is inconsistent" → one 2.00px line weight.
- **10-15 · v6, Constructed chosen:** "the clarity is best with built … the rest risk feeling amateurish. its interesting that your calls have been the exact opposite of mine" → the team's hand-made/novelty bias was named and corrected. `v6-*`
- **10-15 · Taste corrections:** "built can still be whimsical", "never a hand made look is bad", "these are just examples. not exhaustive. again you are overstating my intent." Lesson: quote him, don't harden his words into rules.
- **10-15 · t4 concepts per icon:** "put these in the design canvas i dont need this mocked on a phone" → review moved to the canvas. `icon-concepts/sketches/t4.html`
- **10-15 · MUTCD brown signs added as inspo:** "this gives "built" more legs as a concept" → wit comes from what the object is doing. `design/inspo/mutcd-recreation/`
- **10-15 · t4 picks:** bag, walker, bed, fork and knife; café "need to push here"; bar "not differentiated enough from cafe"; attraction "nordic loop".
- **10-16 · Categories rethought:** "we're uncovering a flaw in the data model" → locked at 11 pins and 2 shapes, using "what am I doing there?" and "would I filter for just these?" Lesson: an icon question can be a data-model question. `categories.md`
- **10-16 · t6 on the canvas:** all 13 side by side, plus the open icons. Asked "which one, or neither?" and where the ⌘ goes.

- **10-16 · t6 picks (ticks on the canvas):** cup and saucer, coupe, column front, ⌘ loop (on Landmark), Ferris wheel, round tree (then bench: "lets do park bench"), conifer. "i still struggle with venue -- it could be music, it could be theatre"
- **10-16 · Venue = curtain:** "again this is an app for way more than just me." Lesson: judge icons for every user, not his current pins.
- **10-16 · All 13 picked:** Neighbourhood "i like the city blocks for neighborhood". "lets not focus on differentiation yet we aren't making final icons here" → on to grid and drawing.
