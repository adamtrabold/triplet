# Icon system: history

One entry per moment the owner saw work or decided. At most 4 lines each, with the deciding quote only. Full words are in `owner.md`, the team's reasoning in the `jam.md` and `scores.md` files. Review canvas: https://claude.ai/artifact/SVKZ9PpaMCKUpk7Ks622jW (older rounds are on its Archive page).

**Snapshots** (`snapshots/`): 1 start: the app today and every icon side by side (the inventory). The end shot gets added when the new icons land.


- **10-07 · The ask:** "they're too noisy … the checked items are stronger visually than the open ones … i need a full rev."
- **10-13 · Every icon side by side:** an inventory of 74 icons on the canvas. `inventory.md`
- **10-14 · False start:** the crew worked from an old backlog line. "Visited should not change. Also I never told you what I wanted changed" and "all I want is every icon side by side" → reset to brand approach first, then grid and SVGs. Lesson: brief from his current words.
- **10-14 · v4 brand directions:** "the pin/place icons need to be solid and outlined icons should be used when something has yet to be tapped by me" → that became the system rule. `brand-approach/concepts/sketches/v4-*`
- **10-15 · The + joins:** "Build the matched icon but we need to include this in the icon project everything is inconsistent" → one 2.00px line weight.
- **10-15 · v6, Constructed chosen:** "the clarity is best with built … the rest risk feeling amateurish. its interesting that your calls have been the exact opposite of mine" → the team's hand-made/novelty bias was named and corrected. `v6-*`
- **10-15 · Taste corrections:** "built can still be whimsical", "never a hand made look is bad", "these are just examples. not exhaustive. again you are overstating my intent." Lesson: quote him, don't harden his words into rules.
- **10-15 · t4 concepts per icon:** "put these in the design canvas i dont need this mocked on a phone" → review moved to the canvas. `icon-concepts/sketches/t4.html`
- **10-15 · MUTCD brown signs added as inspo:** "this gives "built" more legs as a concept" → wit comes from what the object is doing. `design/inspo/mutcd-recreation/`
- **10-15 · t4 picks:** bag, walker, bed, fork and knife; café "need to push here"; bar "not differentiated enough from cafe"; attraction "nordic loop".
- **10-16 · Categories rethought:** "we're uncovering a flaw in the data model" → locked at 11 pins and 2 shapes, using "what am I doing there?" and "would I filter for just these?" Lesson: an icon question can be a data-model question. `categories.md`
- **10-16 · t6 on the canvas:** all 13 side by side, plus the open icons. Asked "which one, or neither?" and where the ⌘ goes.

- **10-16 · t6 picks (ticks on the canvas):** cup and saucer, coupe, column front, ⌘ loop (on Landmark), Ferris wheel, round tree (then bench: "lets do park bench"), conifer. "i still struggle with venue -- it could be music, it could be theatre"
- **10-16 · Venue = curtain:** "again this is an app for way more than just me." Lesson: judge icons for every user, not his current pins.
- **10-16 · All 13 picked:** Neighbourhood "i like the city blocks for neighborhood". "lets not focus on differentiation yet we aren't making final icons here" "we're not at drawing step next, next step is defining the grid"

- **10-16 · How the icons are built:** "they need to sit in the stroked circle but also make sense on their own … the effect of the rounding should allude to something screened or pressed." Full quote in `owner.md`.

- **10-16 · Grid g2 shown:** one frame (a drawing per size, pixel grid, 12px on the 2px module); asked: corner law (by joint, even wear, pressed) and 2px vs 1px of air. Canvas page "Grid concepts". `grid/`

- **10-16 · "I don't see a single grid":** g2 led with icons, and the grid was buried on card 1. Lesson: show the grid as a grid.

- **10-16 · Grid g3 shown:** two grids (A square pixel keyline, B radial seal) with interior guides (TOP, SHOULDER, MIDDLE, BASE, centre axis). Asked: "Which grid do we draw the 13 on". `grid/stills/g3-sheet-3.png`

- **10-16 · A leads, stroke reopened:** "A feels to be producing better sized icons but the icons are too simplistic … I want sub pixels from prolly .8 to 2.5"

- **10-17 · Stroke sweep g4 shown:** 0.8–2.5px, whole family per width (13, UI icons, +, home-screen icon), ink %, buys/costs. Asked: which width. `grid/stills/g4-sheet-1.png`

- **10-17 · Stroke narrowed:** "Feels like 1.5 or 2 to me right now" → a closer look at 1.5–2.

- **10-17 · Drawing batch 1 shown:** café, bar, museum, nature, + and Star drawn at 1.5px on grid A; corner laws and air on real drawings; grid changes 1–8. Asked: closer drawing-wise? which corners, which air. `grid/stills/g6-sheet-1.png`, `g6-sheet-2.png`

- **10-19 · Batch 1 verdict:** drawn side over skeletons, 1.5px confirmed; "none of these are yet at the craft level i need" (part sizes, rounding, interior choices, optical centring and weight). Full quote in `owner.md`.

- **10-19 · Batch 1 redrawn (g7c) shown:** optical centring, balanced weight (blur spread 25→9), ear handle, beam museum, ink traps; beside Material and Phosphor. Asked: craft level as a set? corners? air? `grid/stills/g7-sheet-1.png`

- **10-19 · No ink traps:** "i do not want ink traps... no reason for that on digital imo"

- **10-19 · Review on the canvas only:** "i want them on the icon design board as weve discussed many times over" → no separate pages, no screenshots in place of the canvas.

- **10-20 · Batch 1 on the canvas board (v99):** true-size vectors in rows, map, chips beside Material and Phosphor; corners by joint, 2px air (team calls). Asked: "Do they feel finished to you, as a set? If not, which one looks off?"

- **10-20 · "a *lot* closer":** every meeting point at least a little rounded (signet ring, woodcut, screened banner, neon); cup reads small, coupe tall; "i dont expect or want sweeping changes." Full quote in `owner.md`.

- **10-20 · Board v105 shown:** every meeting point slightly rounded (interior tighter than exterior), cup up, coupe down. Same question: finished as a set?

- **10-20 · Size at the grid level:** v105 comments: root shapes differ (saucer, coupe foot, museum base); coupe still heavy, wants a liquid line; star too big. "i dont want to have to negotiate this all the time, \"how could our grid be improved\" is an overarching question". Quotes in `owner.md`.

- **10-20 · Grid g7i/g7j shown (v115):** size by class ("the token"), five root shapes, Outline class for the Star, stacked-gable tree; Weight board asks: line marks as they are or heavier, and does the coupe match?
