### What blocked a 9 this round, and why

- **The secondary marks** (as predicted): approx dashes (fixed in gx), the
  visited★ specks (Q3), the list stamp (Q2). Each time the visited pin is
  fixed, the next-heaviest modifier becomes the noise, because each mark is
  designed alone and judged together. Fix pattern: judge every change on a
  full lastDay screen, never on a specimen.
- **Motion borrowed without its physics:** the peel moved onto a thing (a
  printed line) that can't peel. When a metaphor moves to a new object, the
  object has to be able to do what the metaphor does.
