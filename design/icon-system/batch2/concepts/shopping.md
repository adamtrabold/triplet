# Shopping: concepts

Job (`../jobs.md`, `../../shopping/jobs.md`): browsing and carrying something home; the most-repeated pin (49), in the weakest ink, so it must be quiet in a crowd and never read as a padlock. No new kind beat the bag in this jam (turn 2: the basket is checkout, the parcel a delivery, the tote a padlock). Declarations: `decl.js` (`shopping`, `sh-carrier`).

## A. The bag (control, `shopping`)

**Declaration:** body: a block the box's full width, as deep as it is wide; handle: a loop on the body's top, 2/3 of its width, its back a half circle on the face, showing through it as slots 1/4 of its depth. Tall class. (9 in one-shot 2.)

**Roots and rules:** block, loop; a handle on a face; detail at 12 (the slots close, the face reads whole).

**How it passes the jobs:** a low handle on a square body: low is what keeps it off the padlock (turn 2's finding: a tall loop on a block is a padlock).

## B. The carrier (`sh-carrier`)

**Declaration:** body: a block the box's full width, 6/5 as deep as it is wide; handle: a loop on its top, 3/5 of its width, on the face, showing through it as slots 1/4 of its depth. Tall class.

**How it differs:** a taller body and a narrower handle: the paper carrier of a record or design shop rather than a square bag. Taller means narrower in the tall class, so it is lighter in a crowd, which suits the most-repeated mark.

**System finding:** it didn't hang; it was slow (see `attraction.md`, B). It now builds.

**Risks:** a narrower handle on a taller body moves toward the fuel can.

## Which, and why (systems)

A, with B to test whether a lighter bag reads as well. The owner's words ("bag is easier to understand at a glance"; "may be one step too common?") are both served only by the bag being ours by construction, which is execution, not subject.
