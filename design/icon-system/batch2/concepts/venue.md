# Venue: concepts

Job (`../jobs.md`): **watching a show**, only when one's on; found in the evening by filter and on the map. Must never be mixed up with Bar (the coupe) or Hotel (a curtain = a bedroom window). Venue owns the drapes (director turn 3). Declarations: `decl.js` (`vn-stage`, `venue`).

## A. The curtain at the stage lip (`vn-stage`)

**What it is:** two drapes gathered back over a stage floor: the moment before the show. The floor is what turns a window into a stage.

**Declaration:** rail: a slab the box's full width; drapes: two drapes a cut apart, each a quarter ellipse to 1/2 of its height, then a post to the bottom, resting on the rail a cut apart; stage: a slab the box's full width, resting on the drapes a cut apart. Square class.

**Roots and rules:** slab, drape, slab; the cut join; a part that must read (the tie stands past the fall by the protrusion minimum). **The engine fix (11-02): "to the bottom" means the bottom of the room the roots below leave.** In turn 2 the drapes ran to the box's bottom and the stage went under that, so the icon grew tall and the fit narrowed it into a door. Now a root that runs to the bottom stops above the fixed roots declared under it (a slab's lines and its cut), so adding the stage changes nothing about the drapes' proportions.

**How it passes the jobs:** F1/F4: a frame of slab top and bottom with an opening between, the drapes' gathered curve the only curve; nothing like a glass. F8: one central opening, large.

**Risks:** a window with a sill (the stage must read as a floor: it's the full width, the same as the rail, which may make it a frame); a gate.

## B. The curtain (control, `venue`)

The one-shot curtain: rail and drapes, tie 3/5. 7 in both one-shots ("the tie too small"), since fixed by "a part that must read". Without a floor it is the window read.

## C. The stub (described, not built)

An admit-one stub torn along a perforated edge. Paper goes to Venue only if the stub survives (director turn 3). It would be a block with a comb of cuts along one side (the comb rule, cut into a mass: a hole grid of one row by the hole rule). Not built: A is the concept; C is kept in reserve if the blind read calls A a window.

A. It keeps the owner-era subject and fixes its one systems fault (the window) with a root, not decoration.
