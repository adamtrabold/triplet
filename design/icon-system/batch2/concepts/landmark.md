# Landmark: concepts

Job (`../jobs.md`): **looking** at something notable, mostly from outside, for a few minutes; found by shape on the map at 18 among Museum, Attraction, Café and Shopping. Must stay off Museum's building front (one cue, one icon). Declarations: `decl.js` (`lm-needle`, `lm-camera`).

## A. The needle (`lm-needle`)

**What it is:** an obelisk on a plinth, the road sign's "monument", no building front.

**Declaration:** tip: a gable over the shaft, pitch 1.6, no ends; shaft: a slab half the base's width, five lines tall; base: a slab the box's full width, two lines thick, resting on the part above, a cut apart. Tall class.

**Roots and rules:** gable, slab, slab; the cut join (the plinth is a separate stone); the apex rule (its point reads by its crest, a fifth of a line). No new rule.

**How it passes the jobs:** F1 (old-town glance): the only icon in the set whose read is **tallness**, so it separates from Museum (wide, columns) and the conifer (tiered) by proportion alone. F6 (12): two solids and a point survive at 12. F8 (reversed): no counters to lose.

**First build (18, seen by me):** with a half-width shaft and the steep tip it reads as a **pencil** standing on a bar. The tip wants to be shorter (an obelisk's pyramidion is low) and the shaft to taper or step.

**Risks:** a pushpin (the thin `lm-obelisk` read as one; the needle's half-width shaft is what holds it off); a pencil. The director's open question: does Landmark want a **slender class** (a box taller and narrower than tall), general for any tall subject? Not built; the tall class is used.

## B. The camera (`lm-camera`)

**What it is:** the owner's "camera says 'sight'" (10-15), now that Landmark is the sight category.

**Declaration:** finder: a block 1/3 of the body wide, 1/3 of its width deep; body: a block the box's full width, 2/3 of its width deep, **a round hole 3/5 of its smaller side, centred across and 11/20 down** (the lens). The finder sits on the body (no cut: one mass). Wide class.

**Roots and rules:** block, block; **a hole in a mass** (new, 11-02): the lens is its own root (a circle) cut through the body, so the solid round it is the camera; corners round the hole are cuts (the rack's 1/8); at 12 the hole closes only if under the counter minimum (it is about 2.5px at 12, so it stays).

**How it passes the jobs:** names the act (looking, taking the picture) for a church, a mural and a square alike. F8: the lens is the one counter, large, so it reads reversed.

**First build (18, seen by me):** reads as a camera at once; the lens is round and centred (after two engine fixes, see `../jam.md` turn 4).

**Risks:** a camera means "take a photo" / "add a photo" in every app; in Triplet it would sit on a pin, never on a button, but a fresh reader may still read "photo spot". The blind read decides.

## C. The medallion (wild card, not built)

**What it is:** the director's tram token: a commemorative coin, a solid disc with the needle cut through it as its counter.

**Why it isn't built:** the hole rule makes round and square holes; a hole in the shape of another root (a gable over a slab) is a further general rule ("a hole may be any root") that only this concept needs. Built only if the director keeps it after A and B are seen.

**Risks:** a ring in the ring badge; a coin means payment (Shopping's ground).

## Which, and why (systems)

A is the safer read and the plainest sign; B is owner-backed and names the act. Both go to the blind read.


## How it looks (visual, 2026-11-03; sheet `../sketches/contact-2.png`, rows `../sketches/rows-2.png`)
- **A. Needle: survives, reshaped.** At pitch 1.6 the tip was a pencil, so I set it to **3/4** (a low pyramidion) and made the shaft 6 lines (declaration only). Now it's an obelisk on a plinth at 18 and at 24, and still a mark at 12. It reads by tallness and by the plinth's weight, not by its tip. The remaining risk is a bottle or a lipstick at 12; the blind read decides.
- **B. Camera: survives, my lead.** It reads at once at 18. The lens is the one big counter, so it holds reversed. It's the owner's own "camera says 'sight'". Beside the café cup it's the only wide mass with a round hole, so nothing else in the set shares its cue.
- **C. Medallion: kill.** A coin in the ring badge is a ring in a ring, and payment is Shopping's ground. Not worth a new rule ("a hole may be any root").
