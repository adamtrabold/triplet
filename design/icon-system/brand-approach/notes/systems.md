## Decisions so far and why

- Ethos, as 8 cited points: collector's keepsake; paper and ink; two voices
  (printed form vs the clerk's stamped entries, owner 2026-10-08); whimsy
  that means something; poster-confident and reductive (few spot inks: the
  Savoy label is literally navy/orange/cream); restraint in effects; colour
  means something; systematic underneath.
- Measured the owner's complaint: on/off pairs differ in mass (filled star
  vs 2px outline; filled stamp check vs stroked mark-visited check). Fill
  vs stroke is split by family. Text glyphs (× ▼) and Leaflet zoom aren't
  ours.
- Five directions, which differ in what kind of object an icon is:
  A Printed & Stamped, B Spot-Colour Pictograms, C Cut Block (stretch),
  D Badge & Emblem, E Code & Flag (wildcard, expected to lose on "preserve
  the icon"). Random draws: Remove specifics / Emphasize differences /
  Retrace your steps; linocut, ISOTYPE, enamel pins, signal flags.
- Eight questions every direction must answer, so the directions are
  comparable and a grid can follow (stroke/fill, corners, weight vs type,
  inks, on/off parity, where texture is allowed, sitting beside visited,
  absorbing the text glyphs and Leaflet zoom).
- Proposed one stress set for all directions: cafe, district, star off/on,
  filters, close ×, + on the scallop. Real sizes, in a row and tag crop,
  beside the visited stamp.
