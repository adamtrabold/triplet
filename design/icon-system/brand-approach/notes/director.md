# Director notes: icon brand approach (phase 1)

## State (after jam turn 3, 2026-10-14)

No scores yet (jam only). Phase 1 = brand attitude for icons, not icons.
Fixed: "Visited should not change."

## Premise decisions (keep consistent)

- The 2026-10-07 quote ("checked items stronger than open") is NOT the
  scope. The owner said "I never told you what I wanted changed".
  Parity is not a requirement. Each direction states its on/off stance as
  a consequence of its attitude. Q1 goes to the owner as a question,
  non-blocking.
- Ethos: "Printed with care, marked by hand, kept like a souvenir. Vintage
  in method, not in costume." Icons are the furniture; the place name is
  the hero (inspo: the furniture is always small, single-ink and quiet).
- A (Printed & Stamped) is a law over all directions. The star sits on the
  seam: the Pencil Star is drawn by hand and lands as print. Not an owner
  question.
- Whimsy budget default: place glyphs plus the user's own marks; tools
  carry none. Stated on the board, overrulable.
