# Icon brand approach: jobs

What this phase has to let you do. It comes from your words of 2026-10-14.
*Inferred* means we added it and you didn't say it.

## Now: the brand approach

1. **See the brand's attitude in plain words**, so every icon can be judged
   against it.
2. **See what supports that attitude and what works against it**, in looks
   and in style.
3. **Choose between more than one direction.** Pick one, several, or a mix.
4. **Give feedback and get a revised direction back.** That's normal
   iteration.
5. *Inferred:* **Judge each direction where icons really live**: in a list
   row (plain and selected), in the tag, on the map, and small (12px). Each
   one sits next to the Visited stamp and sticker, which don't change.
6. *Inferred:* **See every category, not a sample**: all 11 category icons
   side by side in each direction.
7. *Inferred:* **See what's dropped and why**, so you can bring any of it
   back.

## Later, once you approve

8. **The team researches icon best practice** for the chosen direction.
9. **The team builds one shared grid** and draws every icon on it.
10. **Every icon is an SVG**, and you can see them all side by side.
11. **You're told whether a different tool or MCP** would be better for
    drawing.

## Fixed

- **Visited does not change.**
