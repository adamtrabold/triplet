# Icon brand approach: jobs

What this phase has to let you do. It comes from your words of 2026-10-14
and 2026-10-15. *Inferred* means we added it and you didn't say it.
Updated for round 3 (2026-10-15): your solid/outlined rule, the stroked
circle, and the + joining the project.

## Your rules, as we read them

- **Places are solid.** Every place icon (all 11 categories, district and
  street too) is a filled shape, never a line drawing.
- **Places can sit inside a stroked circle.**
- **Outlined means "not tapped by me yet".** Star and Visited are the
  examples: an outlined star waits for you; a filled star is one you made.
- **Visited does not change.**
- **The + is part of the icon project.** It takes whatever line weight the
  project sets ("everything is inconsistent").

## Now: the brand approach

1. **See the brand's attitude in plain words**, so every icon can be judged
   against it.
2. **See what supports that attitude and what works against it**, in looks
   and in style.
3. **Choose between more than one direction.** Pick one, several, or a mix.
4. **Give feedback and get a revised direction back.** That's normal
   iteration.
5. *Inferred:* **Judge each direction where icons really live**: in a list
   row (plain and selected), in the tag, on the map, and small (12px). Each
   one sits next to the Visited stamp and sticker, which don't change.
6. *Inferred:* **See every category, not a sample**: all 11 category icons
   side by side in each direction.
7. *Inferred:* **See what's dropped and why**, so you can bring any of it
   back.
8. **See Field Guide, Cut Paper and Spot Pictogram under your rules**:
   which parts of each survive, and whether they combine into one or stay
   separate choices.
9. **Tell at a glance what you haven't marked yet.** Wherever you can mark
   a place (Star, Visited), the not-yet state is outlined and the done
   state is solid, the same way every time.
10. *Inferred:* **See exactly what counts as "tapped by me"**: a list of
    every on/off icon in the app, which ones follow the outline rule, and
    which don't and why (for example filter chips, the sort tick, the
    selected pin).
11. *Inferred:* **Never mistake a tool for an unmarked place.** If tools
    (sort, filters, locate, ×) are line drawings, they mustn't read as
    "not tapped yet". Each direction says how it keeps the two apart.
12. *Inferred:* **See the stroked circle wherever a place appears**: row,
    map pin, tag, filter chip. Where it's used, where it isn't, and what
    district and street sit in (circle or today's diamond).
13. **See every line in the app at one weight.** The +, account, list
    toolbar, locate and the circles' rims, side by side, today's measured
    weights next to the proposed rule.

## Later, once you approve

14. **The team researches icon best practice** for the chosen direction.
15. **The team builds one shared grid** and draws every icon on it, with
    the line weight set in on-screen pixels so it matches at every size.
16. **Every icon is an SVG**, and you can see them all side by side.
17. **The + (and the account button) take the project's weight** in the
    app.
18. **You're told whether a different tool or MCP** would be better for
    drawing.

## Fixed

- **Visited does not change**: the stamp and the sticker. The outlined
  check you tap to mark a place visited already fits your outline rule, so
  we keep it exactly as it is.
