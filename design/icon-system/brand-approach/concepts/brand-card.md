# Card: The brand, in plain words (words only; visual lays it out)

Goes in front of card 0. Phone-width, short. The copy is below, in order.
Notes for the layout are in [brackets].

---

**THE BRAND, IN PLAIN WORDS**

**Printed with care, marked by hand, kept like a souvenir.**
Triplet should feel like the labels, matchbooks and park posters you bring
home from a trip.

**Vintage in method, not in costume.**
Few inks, cut shapes, real print logic. No fake wear, no old-timey fonts.

**The app prints. You stamp.**
Everything the app shows is printed: clean, flat, the same every time. Only
your own marks, like Visited, look applied by hand.
[A printed label, e.g. "CAFE · REYKJAVÍK", beside the real VISITED stamp,
labelled FIXED]

**Icons are the furniture. The place name is the hero.**
On a luggage label the name is loud and the little pictures are small,
one ink, and quiet.
[One crop: the Savoy label's luggage pictograms]

**Supports**
- One drawing language across every icon
- Few inks, flat colour
- Lightly rounded corners
- Character in the shape, not the surface
- Tools stay quiet
- Every colour means something

**Works against**
- A stock app-icon look
- Lines and solid shapes mixed at random
- Texture, distress or effects inside icons
- Mascots and faces
- Colour that means nothing
- Tools louder than your own marks

[Add a tiny example beside a line only where it helps.]

**The three directions below are three ways to live by this.**

## Where each line comes from (for the team, not on the card)

- **Attitude line:** jam turn 2 and the director's turn 3, from the inspo
  (`design/inspo/README.md`: travel labels, matchbooks, park posters, trail
  ephemera).
- **"Vintage in method, not in costume":** the director's turn 3, from the
  owner's "the typewriter font is unnecessary" and "texture looks like
  clouds".
- **"The app prints. You stamp.":** the owner, 2026-10-08: "The other content
  would be consistent in a printed tag" (only the clerk's entries are
  stamped).
- **"Furniture / hero":** the director's turn 3, from the inspo itself (the
  Savoy label's small pictograms).
- **"Lightly rounded":** the owner's "lightly rounded like the rest of the
  icons".
- **"Same drawing language":** the owner's "Same icon drawing language
  everywhere".
- **"Every colour means something" / "Colour that means nothing":** the
  owner's "Colour must communicate, or go neutral".
- **"Tools stay quiet" / "Tools louder than your own marks":** the owner's
  "secondary marks never outrank content".
- **"Effects":** the owner called shadows too harsh three times.
- **"Mascots and faces" and "A stock app-icon look":** the team's reading
  of the owner's "whimsy, not average" (jam turns 1–2). These are not his
  words, so they aren't quoted on the card.
