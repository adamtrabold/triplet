# L: the open glyph test. Proposed kill, with the reason

**What the director asked (turn 3):** one drawing of Shopping as the set's first open (outline) category glyph, in the street strip, to test whether "most repeated, weakest ink" is a job that earns the open style.

**Why I propose not drawing it:** the owner has already ruled on this. `docs/owner-taste.md`, verbatim: "I think the pin/place icons need to be solid and outlined icons should be used when something has yet to be tapped by me like Star and visited ... and they can be inside a stroked circle" (2026-10-14). In this app an outline means "not yet marked by me". An open Shopping pin would read as a state, and the commonest pin on the map would look switched off. The director expected that in turn 3; the owner's rule makes it certain. Testing it would be reopening his rule, which isn't ours to do.

**What the test was for, and how to get it anyway:** the weight job is real (the commonest pin shouldn't be the heaviest). It can be met inside the solid style:
- pick the lightest concept that reads (K about 66, B1a about 62);
- let the counters carry the lightness (K's handle and crease take out about 13px², and they carry meaning rather than decoration);
- write the weight band as a rule: measure the six's net ink, set a band, and put frequent categories at its light end.

**If the director still wants evidence:** draw L once, labelled "against your 10-14 rule, shown only to settle weight", beside K, inside the team. I wouldn't show it to the owner as an option.
