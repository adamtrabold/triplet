## Decisions I made in the jam (and why)
- Reading: the corner badge IS the sign-in button; no dropdown when signed
  out. (Owner's arc idea puts the words on the badge; menu has nothing left
  to say.)
- Secondary grammar = same 50px scallop die, paper face, navy print. Orange
  = signed-in only. Shared by all concepts.
- 50px die kept, person shrinks (~10px), arc type 8px (visual's sketch fact).
- KILLED the ±2° seeded tilt: tilted round navy ink with arc caps = rubber
  stamp = VISITED. Sign-in is "the form's own words" -> printed, crisp.
- Pushed on the scallop keyline: too much edge activity vs the `+`; asked for
  thin/no-keyline variants on cream and dark tiles.
- Concepts forward: A arc badge (owner's), B printed die-cut label (benchmark;
  grows leftward only, + never moves). Two, not more.
- In scope: rest/pressed/focus, signed-in pair, 320/390, light/dark tiles,
  F1b flow (modal over playground; success -> real app), 3-4 frame
  storyboards both directions (motion plays on arrival in real app, or in
  place if owner Q1 = yes; reuse star/visited timing; out = dry-up by
  thickness).
- Out: register-styled sheet, playground notice, F1c, modal restyle.
- Parked: initial (A/E) on the signed-in badge — one-line "later?" to owner
  at most.
