Un-visit Transition

Four ways for the VISITED stamp to leave when you tap Visited again on the tag. Each strip runs top to bottom, from before the tap to the resting “Mark visited”. Pinch to zoom.

1. Lift off

The stamp-in, played backwards. The stamp gives the same small lift the star does when you unstar it, then rises off the paper along the path it came down (turning back, growing, softening) and is gone. 340 ms.

2. Dry up

The ink bleed of the list swipe, reversed. After the same small lift, the ink shrinks back from the ring’s ends toward its centre through the paper’s grain, paling as it goes. 380 ms.

3. Rub out

The Pencil Star’s erase. After the small lift, the stamp is rubbed out in three back-and-forth strokes, left to right, and a few eraser crumbs are swept into the corner. 400 ms.

4. Strike through

How a clerk cancels a stamp: one pencil stroke is drawn through it, then stamp and stroke fade together. No lift. 380 ms.

For comparison: today

The stamp fades and grows a little over 220 ms, with “Mark visited” already showing underneath from the first frame.

Reduced motion (all four)

With reduced motion turned on, every option becomes the same plain 160 ms crossfade: no lift, no movement.
