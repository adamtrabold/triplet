Star Colour & Tag Paper

The gold star, with the two clashing categories recoloured. Pinch to zoom.

1. Gold star everywhere

Gold #F2B807, the yellow ink of your tags, with a thin black outline so it reads on any paper. It means “starred” and nothing else. It sits well clear of the orange clusters and selected rows in every city. On a selected (orange) row the star turns paper, as today.

2. Categories that clash, recoloured

Restaurant (orange) and attraction (gold) collided with a gold star. Restaurant becomes claret, attraction becomes moss green. Everything else stays. No two categories end up closer than before.
