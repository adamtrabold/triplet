The 5 biggest mismatches

  1. The address is the heaviest thing and the least needed. Your addresses are full OSM strings (avg 103 characters, 135 of 202 over 90) so they take 3 lines. It carries 2.4x the ink of the name.
  2. Your notes are tier 1 but styled as fine print. They're the only place your reasons appear in the whole app, yet they're small grey italic, under the address, with line breaks lost.
  3. Type is at the bottom, ~235px below the name in a full popup. Where it goes is open now; it should read lighter than the name and notes. The popup icon is also still bare while the pin and row show it in a ring.
  4. Spacing follows invisible tap boxes, not the 4px scale. Gaps you can see: 3, 10, 25, 37, 42px. A name-only popup is 162px tall and about 70% empty.
  5. Five controls in four corners. Star top-left, × top-right, Directions middle-left, Visited bottom-right. Directions is the only coloured thing, so it shouts; Mark Visited (the trip's other key action) is the quietest.

Spacing

A system already exists: 4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 24 / 32 / 48px, plus a 28px icon column that rows, the header and forms all share. The popup ignores both:

  • Visible gaps: name→address 3, address→notes 10, notes→Directions 25, Directions→type 37, name→Directions 42 (bare). Cause: the CSS spaces the 44px tap boxes, not the words.
  • Tight inside blocks, huge between them, so it reads as three islands.
  • Two left edges (star/icon column ~13px, words at 36 vs name at 38), neither on the app's 28px column.

Rules any new design must keep

  1. Get Directions and Mark Visited: one tap from the open place, 44px targets, 8px between targets.
  2. Star reachable in one tap from the place (VoiceOver's only path); every star/visit target keeps the haptic tap label.
  3. Toggling never closes the place or moves the map; the list row replays.
  4. The tapped pin stays visible and clearly linked; top controls stay clear.
  5. List tap → map flies → place opens, the same for pins, districts, streets and approx pins (same content for every kind).
  6. A 44px close plus tap-the-map to close.
  7. The glance layer never covers the whole map.
  8. Typical notes readable at first glance, no tap; only very long ones fold (full text ≤1 tap). Full address reachable.
  9. Anything outside the popup (tab, side or bottom sheet) must not fight the list sheet, its swipes, Safari's edge back-swipe, or change filters / scroll / selection.
  10. No marks for empty data ("no star unless starred" for marks; how the off-star button reads is the designer's call).

Other things I found

  • Tapping a pin on the map doesn't select it (no highlighted pin or row); tapping it in the list does. Same place, two states.
  • Closing the popup keeps the selection highlighted.
  • Directions is always driving mode (walking trip).
  • "(approx.)" appears in the name and again as a sentence in the notes.
  • The popup's VISITED dot is a third visited look, next to the row stamp and the pin sticker.
