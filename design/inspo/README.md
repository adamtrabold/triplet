# Inspiration images

Reference images shared during design work, saved here since they came in as
chat uploads and don't otherwise persist anywhere durable.

## `project/`

Overarching visual direction for the app: vintage travel labels (hotel
luggage labels, matchbook collections) and vintage national/state park
posters and trail ephemera. This is the aesthetic language the app's design
tokens (paper/ink/figure-deep colors, ledger-row layout, stamp/label motifs)
draw from.

## `visited-badge/`

Reference specifically for the sidebar "VISITED" stamp badge (see
`../visited-badge/NOTES.md`): passport arrival/departure stamps — ticket-cut
outlines, dotted/dashed borders, bold condensed stamp typography. The
annotated Tokyo stamp crop is the one most directly cited while iterating on
the badge shape.

## `luggage-tags/`

Owner's reference for the popup's Hanging Tag (2026-10-06, round 5): vintage
airline and railway baggage tags and claim checks. `1.jpg` a vector set
(LAX, BERLIN, NYC: big destination codes, labelled FLIGHT / DATE / TO
fields, strap and claim check numbers); `2.jpg` Japan Air Lines, Aloha and
Hawaiian tags (huge three-letter codes, a letter in the corner, serial
numbers in a band at the foot, tear-off claim stubs, string); `3.webp` a
spread of Delta, Braniff, National tags (codes, small labelled fields, serial
numbers); `4.webp` Air Midwest / Braniff tags (STANDBY, GATE BAGGAGE, LATE
CHECK-IN: one big word, labelled fields with arrows, perforation notches);
`5.webp` Southern Pacific railway checks (eyelets with brown reinforcement
patches, "No. of Pieces" fields, From / To lines, ruled fields, a big serial
number). Common grammar: one big destination word, small labelled fields in
a clear order, perforated tear-off claim stubs, reinforced eyelets, string,
serial numbers.
