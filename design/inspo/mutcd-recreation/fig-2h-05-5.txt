Figure 2H-5. Recreational and Cultural Interest Area Symbol Signs 
(Sheet 5 of 5)

Sheet 5 of the figure shows four signs.

RS-070 is shown with a symbol of a person driving a snowmobile. 
It is labeled "Snowmobiling."
RS-080 is shown with a symbol of a person with one foot raised 
walking in snowshoes. It is labeled "Snowshoeing."
RS-090 is shown with a large snowflake. It is labeled "Winter 
Recreation Area."
RS-100 is shown with a symbol of a person wearing skis and sitting 
on a chairlift. It is labeled "Chairlift."
