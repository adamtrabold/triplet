V1 · Corner badge

Star is a small badge pinned to the row's own top-right corner (position:absolute, top:-1px), like a notification dot on an app icon. It floats OVER the row, never inside its box model — the grid, the h3's available width, and the row-meta's available width are byte-identical whether a row is starred or not. A 2px paper/filed ring keeps it legible against either field.
