# C · The city you're in

**The city you've gone to is the bucket you're filling.** Make "Philadelphia" (New city, typed in
the title menu, like New plan) and you're in it; every stop you add that isn't near another of
your cities files under Philadelphia, however far out. The city's own Rename / Move centre /
Delete city sit at the foot of its section, like a plan's foot.

Stills: `stills/6-C-the-city-youre-in.png` (raws `C1`..`C3`).

## Draws on

- **Twist drawn at random: "the place picks the city, not the other way round" — inverted:** the
  city you're working in picks the place.
- **Mushroom foraging** (random draw): you carry one basket for the walk; everything you find goes
  in it until you put it down.
- **The Plans pattern already shipped**: "New plan" row → "New city" row; the plan foot (Rename,
  Delete plan…) → the city foot.

## The moments

1. **New city in the menu.** Title menu, Places side: "+ [Philadelphia] CREATE" inline, as New plan
   does. Creating goes to it.
2. **You're in Philadelphia.** The list title and section say so; an empty city's section reads
   "Nothing here yet. While you're in Philadelphia, what you add files here, wherever it is."; its
   foot: RENAME · MOVE CENTRE · DELETE CITY….
3. **A far stop files here.** Cape Henlopen (130 km, near none of your cities) reads PHILADELPHIA,
   "≈ 130 km from Philadelphia", instead of NEW Lewes. The menu still offers "+ Lewes".

## Rules

- **The guess:** a city within 70 km → it (unchanged); else **the city you're in** (the list title's
  city; not when it is All places); else the real town NEW.
- The add menu keeps B's "+ Name a city…" line too (places-first: you don't have to make the city
  first).
- Move centre uses B's fixed-signpost move mode; delete uses the same choices as B, inline in the
  list.

## Weak spots

- **Invisible state.** The bucket depends on what the list title last said. Go to Philadelphia,
  forget, and an Iceland stop with no city in reach files under Philadelphia (the distance note
  shows it, the menu fixes it, but it's a surprise).
- The foot under the section is more chrome in the list; the city still has no place to be *read*.
