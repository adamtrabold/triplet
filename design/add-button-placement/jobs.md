# + placement: jobs and flows

What the design has to let you do. If any of this is wrong or missing, say
so.

## Jobs

1. **Reach + with your thumb, one-handed.** The list is up or down, a tag
   is open, you're in Plans or the filters are open: it doesn't matter, and
   you never have to shift your grip.
2. **See + straight away as the thing you do here.** It's the main action,
   not one of a pair with the account button.
3. **Finish adding near your thumb too.** *(inferred)* The form's Add button
   and its close are easy to reach as well, at any phone height, including
   Safari with its toolbars showing.
4. **Add without losing your place.** *(inferred)* If you cancel, the list,
   its scroll, the selected place and any open tag are just as you left
   them.
5. **+ never gets in the way of your trip.** *(inferred)* It never sits on a
   pin, a tag, a place's name or a row's delete ×.
6. **It makes sense signed out and in the playground.** *(inferred)* Signed
   out, tapping + asks you to sign in. In the playground (`?play`), it just
   adds.

## Flows

**Add a place**
1. Tap + at the right end of the list header.
2. The list slides down to its bottom bar and the + (now ×) rides down with
   it. The add form sits right on that bar, with ADD LOCATION just above
   the ×, and the map shows above the form.
3. Search, pick, then tap ADD LOCATION.
4. The form closes and the list comes back where it was.
   *(See question 2: the map could also go to the new place.)*

**Cancel**: tap × (where + was). The list comes back exactly as it was.

**List already down**: tap + in the bottom bar. Nothing moves: the form
opens above it.

**Filters open**: tap +. The filters close first, then the form opens.

**A tag open**: tap +. The form opens; if you cancel, the tag is still open
on the same place.

**Plans**: + adds a place, as it does today.

**Signed out**: tap + and the sign-in sheet opens. **Playground**: tap + and
the form opens.

**Computer (wide screen)**: + sits at the right end of the list's header in
the side panel.
