## The lifted pin at rest

- **Disc:** a 48 face in `--brand-orange`, a 1.5 `--brand-orange-deep` rim and a 2px `--paper` halo (52 overall).
  - The navy + is 0.22 × D from the centre (21px arms) at a 3.4 stroke, with round caps.
  - The disc casts one soft lift shadow (0 5px 4px, ink 10%), the "held above the map" cue. It's the only shadow.
- **Needle, not string:** a navy wedge tapering from 2.2px at the disc to 0.6px at the point, 22px long.
  - It tapers so it reads as a pin's point going in, not a lollipop stick (a stick is even and blunt). This was changed from the turn-34 sketch's even 1.6px line after looking at it at 3x.
- **Ground point:** a 12×3.8 ellipse at 15% `rgba(31,47,74)` at the needle's tip. It's quieter than the halo, as the director asked.
- **Place:**
  - ink 19.9 from the left;
  - the ground point 20 above the list;
  - box 17.9-69.9 × 450-528 at 844.
  - It rides the sheet's edge (`P5`).
- **The account badge** sits alone at `right: var(--s4)`.

## States

- **Pressed:** the face goes deep orange and the + paper (the shared pressed grammar of orange actions, as on A).
- **Tap:** the pin rises to the dock and shrinks 52 → 32. The needle and shadow fade over the first 100ms, and the string appears as the tag drops (the tag-pop timing). It reads as the pin lifting the tag out of the ground.
- **Hidden** (a place's tag open, filters open, while adding): no animation, just gone. It's back when the tag closes.
- **After ADD:** when the place's tag closes, the fresh lifted pin is simply there (no fly-in, no pop). That's the director's baggage-drop exit, made quiet.

## The 1x kill test, read plainly (`P2`)

- **Not a FAB:** at 1x the needle and ground point break the floating-disc silhouette. The stock FAB beside it reads as a button; P reads as a pin about to go in. In the 4x close-up the difference is plain: the FAB hovers on a wide soft shadow, while P stands on a point.
- **The honest catch:** blurred (the squint), the needle disappears. Rank comes from the disc alone (1.40x the account, systems' number). So "pin" is a 1x read, not a squint read, and that's the right way round: the squint is about rank, the 1x look is about meaning.
- **Lollipop risk:** low at 22px with a tapered needle; it would rise with a longer or even-width stick. Keep 22 and the taper.
- **"Bigger than every other pin":** it is (48 vs 24/32). The lift is the reason it can be: it's the pin in your hand, not one on the map. Once placed it's 32 like any selected pin (T3).
