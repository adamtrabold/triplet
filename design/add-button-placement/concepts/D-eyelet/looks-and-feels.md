# D. Eyelet: the one keep-or-kill frame (visual designer)

Frame: `D-eyelet-tag-open-3x.png` (390x844, starred tag open, list up), plus
`D-eyelet-rest-3x.png`. Rendered from this folder's `index.html` copy
(`node render.js`).

Look drawn: a 44px brand-orange disc straddling the centre of the sheet's top
edge. Its paper ring is the tag's grommet ring, with a navy + and the tag's
restrained shadow.

**Verdict: kill.** In one line: centred on the sheet's top edge it reads as
Material's docked FAB or the sheet's drag knob, it covers the OSM credit
(licence), and it crowds the title's count. The tag gap is fine at 844 (the
tag ends 175px above it), but that doesn't save it. No owner question.
