## What's new for A in this package

- **Open state while adding:** the cut is navy with a paper × (round 7's ON state). It stays on the band at the bottom while the tag hangs (`A3` frames 3-4), as a second cancel. It turns back to the orange + once the place is added (frame 5).
- **The pull-up motion:** the blank tag rises out of the cut. It starts as the cut's own shape (chamfers left, 60 × 57), grows to the 316 tag and travels to the dock in one tag-pop beat. The 32px blank pin and its string draw in at the top as the tag passes the band, so rule 1 holds from the first frame where the tag is free of the header. Cancel reverses it, back down into the cut.
- **Two oranges with a starred tag open** (round 7, accepted) are unchanged by the form: the blank tag is cream until STAR.
- **Float frame (`A5`):** round 7's 60px orange scalloped badge at the bottom-left. While adding, it's navy with a paper ×, like A's cut. The same form, the same pin.
