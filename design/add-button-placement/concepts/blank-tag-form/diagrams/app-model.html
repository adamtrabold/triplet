2 · Who can change things

flowchart TD
  O["Open the app"] --> Q{"Signed in?"}
  Q -->|"yes: Adam or Erica"| R["Real database
reads and writes"]
  Q -->|"no"| G["Playground
a copy in memory"]
  G -->|"add, star, visit,
delete all work"| X["Nothing is saved:
a reload starts fresh"]
  G -->|"account badge → Sign in"| R
  R -->|"anyone may read"| V["Public read"]

Signed out, you're in the playground: the app loads a copy of everything and lets you change it, but nothing reaches the database. Only the two signed-in owners can save. Signing in or out reloads the app into the other mode.

4 · What a tap does

flowchart LR
  R["Tap a row"] --> F["Map flies to it,
zooms in far enough"] --> T["Its Hanging Tag opens"]
  P["Tap a pin"] --> T
  T --> SEL["The row turns navy
(selected)"]
  T -->|"close the tag"| CLR["Selection clears"]
  C["Tap a cluster"] --> Z["Map zooms in
to separate the pins"]

Every list item goes to its place on the map, from any zoom, and opens its tag when it arrives. The selected row follows whichever tag is open.

flowchart LR
  ROW["A row"] -->|"stroke right"| STAR["Pencil Star:
star / unstar"]
  ROW -->|"stroke left"| VIS["Visited stamp:
visit / un-visit"]
  ROW -->|"tap ×"| DEL["Delete"]
  TAG["The tag"] -->|"Directions"| AM["Opens Apple Maps"]
  TAG -->|"Star"| STAR
  TAG -->|"Visited"| VIS

Star and visited can be set from the row with a stroke, or from the tag with a tap. Both write to the same place, so the pin, the row and the tag all change together. Directions hands off to Apple Maps.

  CC["Pick a city chip"] --> AC["Active city changes"]

Filters decide what's in the list and on the map; zoom only decides what the map can draw, never what the list shows. Sort reorders the list only. Unsure: exactly how "show my location" and the "nearest" sort hand off to each other.

6 · Adding a place (today's form)

flowchart TD
  B["Tap +"] --> AU{"Allowed?"}

Search starts in the city the map is showing and only looks further if it finds nothing. The city is filled in from where the picked place is. A place outside all your cities offers to create a new city.

Districts and streets go through the same form but are traced from OpenStreetMap. If there's no outline, the app saves an approximate pin instead and says so in its name.

8 · Saving and syncing

sequenceDiagram
  participant You as Your phone
  participant DB as Database
  participant Her as Erica's phone
  You->>You: star / visit shows at once
  You->>DB: save the change
  DB-->>You: on error, undo on screen
  Her->>DB: checks every 10 s (60 s when idle)
  DB-->>Her: the new mark appears

Changes show on your screen straight away and then save. If a save fails, the screen goes back. The other phone picks up changes by checking every 10 seconds while in use, or every minute when idle.

Proposed · not built

Groups vs. real cities

Today "city" means two things at once. The proposal splits them: the group is how you think about the trip, and the real city is where the place actually is.

flowchart LR
  PL["A place"] -->|"you choose
(filled in for you)"| GR["Group
e.g. Chicago"]
  PL -->|"from the address"| RC["Real city
e.g. Oak Park"]
  GR --> LS["The list you look at"]
  RC --> CF["A new 'City' filter
inside the group"]

A place lives in the group you put it in, even when its address is somewhere else. Its real city stays as data, for the address line and for filtering.

flowchart TD
  G["Group: Chicago"]
  G --> W["Wrigley Field
real city: Chicago"]
  G --> F["Frank Lloyd Wright Home
real city: Oak Park"]
  G --> B["Bahá'í House of Worship
real city: Evanston"]
  CF["City filter in the Chicago group:
Chicago · Oak Park · Evanston"] -.-> G

All three live in your Chicago group. The City filter narrows the group to one real city when you want it.

In every case the group is filled in for you and you can tap it to change it. Search looks everywhere, with places near the group you're looking at listed first.
